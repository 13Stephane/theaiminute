Will this AI vendor still matter in two years?

AI split business software into three layers: the model, which everyone rents; the interface, which is cheap to copy; and the data and learning, which improve with use. Four questions show which layer a vendor owns, and which one you do.

1Which layer does the vendor own?

Pick what it actually holds, not what the pitch deck says.

2Could a large platform ship this in one release?

Think of the suites your company already pays for.

3Who holds your corrections, and who learns from them?

Every time your people fix an output, someone's system gets better.

4If the vendor disappears, what leaves with it?

Acquisition, shutdown, or a price you won't pay.
