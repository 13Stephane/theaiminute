Where the value sits

Theories of value

  • Operational efficiency: the primary thesis. $2.2 bn of cost synergies, the 3G playbook.
  • Capital redeployment: buying growth AB InBev could not build; Baldwin's own account of 3G (case, p. 6).
  • Intangible capital: brands are protected IP; recipes are not. Craft beer takes share at the margin (case, p. 5).
  • Regulatory & political: disposals in the US, China and Europe decide how much of the target survives.
  • Risk & resilience: leverage, and emerging-market revenue against dollar debt.

Teece: who keeps the spread

  • Complementary assets: SAB's routes to market in Africa and Latin America; partnerships with Castel and Coca-Cola bottlers (data pack, slide 2). AB InBev cannot build these quickly.
  • Appropriability: brands can be protected; cost savings cannot. Rivals can copy procurement and overhead savings.
  • The holder of the scarce asset keeps the spread: in a contested auction that is the target's shareholders, through the premium.
