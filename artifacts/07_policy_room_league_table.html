How the score works. Team stabilisation loss = average inflation gap from 2% + end-of-game unemployment above natural + output-gap distance, for both blocs, plus penalties for average financial stress and any euro-area spread above 1pp; mapped to 0–100. Each role is scored against its own mandate (Fed: US inflation + unemployment; ECB: euro-area inflation + the spread; US Gov: recovery without overheating or excess debt; EU Govs: recovery + spread discipline). Scored against stabilisation, never against matching what actually happened — reality wasn’t optimal. A rigorous individual-contribution measure (counterfactual re-runs) can be added if you want true attribution.
