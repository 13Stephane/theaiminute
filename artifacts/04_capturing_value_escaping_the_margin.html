Escaping the margin

Competition drags price down to the marginal cost of the last unit — that is commoditization, the marginal-pricing mechanism working on you. Price detaches from cost and climbs toward total worth only when you break one of three conditions: fungibility, arbitrage, or free entry. Break a lever and watch your captured value rise.

The AI edge. AI pushes the marginal cost of cognitive work toward zero — so any business whose output AI can replicate is being commoditized, hard. The escape is unchanged: stop being a fungible, arbitrageable, freely-entered good.
