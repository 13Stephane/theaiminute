Your table's hypothesis

Ten minutes. One position your table will defend. Including what you couldn't agree on.

The question
Over the next 3–5 years, where in the software industry will the most new value be created — and what bottleneck makes it accrue there?
New value, not existing market share. Where is the wedge opening, not who's winning today.

What "good" looks like — two sample team hypotheses

Hypothesis
The most new value will be created at the agentic orchestration layer that composes vertical capabilities on demand — neither hyperscalers nor today's SaaS incumbents capture it.
Bottleneck
Cross-system trust and reliability in multi-step agent composition. Whoever holds the orchestration graph holds the rents.
Falsifier
If foundation models absorb orchestration natively and the layer commoditizes within 18 months — e.g. if GPT-6 or Claude 5 ship reliable multi-step agentic composition out of the box by mid-2027.
Did not converge
Two members held that vertical SaaS still captures more new value because workflow embedding takes years and orchestration is fragile in regulated domains.
Hypothesis
The most new value will be created by AI-native challengers attacking regulated verticals (clinical trials, legal discovery, drug safety) where incumbent SaaS has 15 years of debt and is too slow to rebuild.
Bottleneck
Speed of regulated-domain rebuild from a clean slate using foundation models — incumbents cannot rewrite the stack without breaking existing customers.
Falsifier
If no AI-native challenger in healthcare or legal-tech raises a Series C above $200M by end of 2027, the wedge is not real.
Did not converge
One member argued incumbents will simply acquire the strongest challengers before they reach scale.
One sentence. Where will the most new value be created over the next 3–5 years, and why? State it so clearly that the table next to you could disagree with it.
What stays scarce — the point of control that determines who captures the new value. What cannot be commoditized, what stays expensive, what's hard to substitute.
Ask whether the bottleneck you named is the binding one or a downstream symptom.
Required. Pre-register the specific evidence that would make your table abandon this hypothesis. Specific enough to be observable — name a date, a measurable threshold, an event you'd see in the news.
Get feedback on whether your falsifier is specific enough to be observable.
Required. What did your table disagree on? Whose argument did not win, and what was it? If there was no disagreement, write "no dissent surfaced" — but interrogate that.
Surfaces 2–3 sharp questions a serious dissenter would raise. Your table decides which ones a member actually held.
